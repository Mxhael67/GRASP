CREATIVE ECONOMY

ONYX

ONYX is a fashion brand created as a creative business mock-up that reflects the creative economy. The brand focuses on fashion and design while combining creativity, identity, and business.

Fashion becomes more than clothing. It becomes a way of expressing ideas, culture, personality, and creativity.

ONYX represents the idea that creativity can become a product, a business, and an opportunity.

FIGURE 01

Lunar Mist Dress

A soft and elegant fashion concept designed with a sophisticated style. The design represents creativity and individuality through fashion.

The flowing design creates a graceful visual effect while the details give the piece its own identity.

This concept demonstrates how fashion can combine visual design and personal expression to create something unique.

FIGURE 02

ONYX Taupe Suit

A modern formalwear concept that combines a clean silhouette with strong visual details.

The design demonstrates how fashion can transform creative ideas into products that can have economic value.

It represents a balance between professionalism, creativity, and modern fashion.
